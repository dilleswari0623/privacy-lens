About Privacy Lens


Privacy Lens is a simple privacy analysis project that helps users understand important information mentioned in app privacy policies.

It identifies privacy-related factors such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, device data, tracking and personal information.

It also provides a simple policy explanation and allows users to compare two app policies.
